[[SOURCES#^b07861|SOURCE]] #Methods

1. Detect the silhouettes, contours, and boundary edges.
2. Generate feature lines but remove occluded line segments.
3. Create color and lighting reference images.
4. Let the user sketch a few hatching carriers.
5. Automatically grow thousands of hatching strokes inside matching regions.
6. Smooth those paths into curves.
7. Convert curves into pencil strokes with thickness, pressure, and tip simulation.
8. Render them onto a synthetic paper surface using a graphite deposition model.
